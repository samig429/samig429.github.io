Shy Guinea Pig Blind Books

Welcome to Shy Guinea Pig Blind Books! We believe that sometimes the best stories are the ones you never expected to read. Each book is carefully wrapped with only a few hints, allowing you to experience the excitement of discovering something completely new.

Why Blind Books?

Blind books turn reading into discovry. No more choosing a book based on the cover, popularity, or author. Now you get to choose based off what YOU like, based off tropes, subjects, etc. This encourages curiosity, broadens reading horizons, and creates a sense of excitement that traditional book shopping can't.

Whether you're looking for your next favorite novel, a gift, or a new experience, Shy Guinea Pig Blind Books offers a fun and meaningful way for you to connect with stories. Every wrapped book will be chosen with care, making each one of them feel personal from the moment it's opened.
