Store Hours

Shy Guinea Pig Blind Books is open throughout the week for readers who want to explore new stories and discover unexpected favorites. Our cozy shop provides a relaxing environment where customers can browse mystery books, ask for recommendations, and enjoy the surprise of a wrapped book.

Our team carefully prepares every blind book package before opening each day. We recommend visiting during quieter hours if you would like help choosing a personalized surprise based on your reading preferences.

Special Events

Shy Guinea Pig Blind Books hosts special events throughout the year to bring readers together. Guests can participate in book discussions, surprise unwrapping events, and activities designed for book lovers of all ages.

Our events are created to celebrate curiosity and encourage readers to explore stories outside of their usual favorites. Every gathering is a chance to connect with other readers and discover something new.
